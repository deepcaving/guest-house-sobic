# The host's English card

Print this on A4, laminate it, and pin it inside the kitchen-cabinet door.
This is the single cheapest thing in the whole marketing folder: it removes
the awkwardness of every interaction with a guest who cannot speak English,
without her having to learn anything.

**How to use it:** she points at a line and says it. The guest reads it. It
works in both directions — the guests can point back too.

**Pronunciation** is written in plain English syllables. It is a guide, not a
transcript — she will not sound native and does not need to. The guest
understands a careful person trying; what they do not understand is silence.

## Notes

- **The numbers are the hard part in English.** "Seven", "six", "eight",
  "forty-five" — write those on a separate small card near the Wi-Fi password
  if the first week goes badly.
- **Print the "river rules" section twice** and put one copy in each bedroom
  and one in the dining hall. It is the legal protection as well as the
  communication: the cold-water rules should be in writing, repeated at
  check-in, and posted where people will see them.
- **Add the real town names** for the doctor and the shop before printing —
  placeholders are useless on the wall.
- **Add the real Wi-Fi name and password** to the first card. That is the
  single most-looked-at line on the whole thing.

## The safety rules, in the guest's language

Post this in the dining hall and put it in every booking confirmation. It is
not a courtesy — one cold-water incident would end a year of revenue.

> The Pliva is a spring creek at 8 °C all year.
> • No swimming outside the property. The water is far too cold.
> • Life vests are worn at all times on the guided days.
> • A wetsuit and buoyancy aid are essential for wading. We provide both.
> • Light rafting is summer only, for guests aged 10 and over, with an adult
>   supervising and a safety briefing beforehand.
> • Catch and release is mandatory on the Pliva.
>
> We will always show you these on the first morning. Please ask us anything.

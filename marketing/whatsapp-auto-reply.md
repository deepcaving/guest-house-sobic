# WhatsApp Business — the channel that actually gets answered

Set up in **WhatsApp Business** (business.whatsapp.com, or the Business app on
the phone) → **Settings → Business tools → Away message**.

This is the primary contact route on the website, so it is worth 15 minutes of
setup.

---

## 1. Away message (set this first)

> Hi! Thanks for contacting Pliva River Lodge 🏞️
> We reply within 24 hours — usually much sooner.
>
> To speed things up, please send:
> 1. Your preferred dates
> 2. How many people
> 3. Are you fly fishing, or just visiting?
> 4. Where you'll fly into (Sarajevo / Banja Luka / Split / driving)
>
> In the meantime, our packages and rates are here: {{SITE_URL}}
>
> Pliva River Lodge, Šipovo, Republika Srpska 🇧🇦

**Set it to send after 15 minutes** of inactivity, not instantly — an instant
reply to every "hi" looks like a bot and the conversation dies there.

## 3. The triage flow

When a message comes in, four questions get you everything you need to quote:

1. **Dates** — and how flexible
2. **How many** — and how many are fishing
3. **Airport** — Sarajevo, Banja Luka, Split, or driving
4. **Fishing or not** — which decides The Pliva Week vs The Companion Week

Ask them in one message, not four. Then quote the whole thing in one reply:
package, price per person, what's included, what isn't, and the balance due
on arrival.

**Answer within 24 hours. Ideally within two.** A lodge that replies the same
evening wins guests that a lodge that replies in three days loses. If you
cannot answer properly, acknowledge it the same day and promise a time.

## 4. Things to send proactively

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| Booking confirmed | the booking confirmation email + a short WhatsApp "you're in, here's the plan" |
| 7 days before | the pre-arrival pack, condensed into a message |
| 2 days before | "Flight landed? We'll be at arrivals at [time] with a name board." |
| On arrival day | "You're on the river for tomorrow, we'll meet at 06:45 with coffee" |
| After the stay | the review request |
| 3 months after | "The BWO hatch starts next month. We have the September week open — shall I hold a room?" |

The last one is the most valuable and almost nobody does it. The autumn is
the highest-demand window of the year, and the guest who fished in June is the
guest who books September.

---

## 5. The catalogue

Set up a WhatsApp Business **catalogue** with the three packages as items and
the room rate as a fourth. When a guest asks "what do you have?", send the
catalogue link instead of typing prices. It saves you ten minutes a time and
it looks professional.

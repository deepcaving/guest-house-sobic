### How to post in them

**Do not post an ad.** These groups ban promotional posts on sight, and
suspension is permanent. The order that works:

1. **Week 1 — observe.** Join, read the rules, scroll. Like and comment on
   other people's catches. No posting at all for seven days.
2. **Week 2 — be useful.** Answer 5–10 questions about Bosnia, the Pliva,
   fishing there, hatches, travel. Be the person who knows. Put the link in
   your profile, never in the post.
3. **Week 3 — post once, as a story.** A trip report. First person. Three
   photos. End with a question, not a call to action.
4. **Week 4+ — one post a week in two or three groups**, and a DM to anyone
   who replies to you.

**The trip-report post** (the one that works):

> Just got back from a few days at a small riverside guesthouse on the Pliva in
> Bosnia I hadn't heard of before. Two kilometres below the source, on the
> bank — a wood-fire dining hall, a terrace over the water, seven rooms. The
> cook put a Pliva trout on the grill and honestly it might have been the best
> fish I've eaten.
>
> The dry-fly fishing was exactly what people say it is — gin-clear water,
> trophy grayling, slow and technical. Not the easiest river I've fished but
> probably the most beautiful.
>
> Photos: [1. riverside terrace with sofas] [2. dining hall with fireplace]
> [3. the river bend at golden hour]
>
> Has anyone else stayed there? Or am I late to this one?

Put the link **in the first comment**, not in the post. Every one of these
groups consistently downvotes anything that smells like an ad, and
consistently upvotes genuine "I just discovered this" stories.

## The three habits that decide whether this works

1. **Answer every message within 24 hours.** A lodge that answers the same
   evening beats a lodge with better photos.
2. **Never advertise in a group. Be useful in it.** Answer someone's
   question about the Pliva, then wait. The link is in your bio.
3. **One post a week beats five posts one week.** The market rewards
   consistency. A gap of three months is worse than a mediocre post.

## The three things not to do

1. **Don't run Facebook ads yet.** Not until there are at least 10 Google
   Business Profile reviews and a clear offer. Paid ads without social proof
   is money into a void.
2. **Don't open a separate company for this.** The marketing happens under
   the lodge's own name and her own social accounts. If the property pays
   anyone, invoice for it separately.
3. **Don't wait for the perfect moment to post.** The first post will be
   slightly the wrong crop. The blog post will have a typo. Post it anyway.

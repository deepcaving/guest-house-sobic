### The Ribnik (the second river)

- 50 km north-west, **about an hour** by car. Daily transport is arranged at
  reception for **€80 per group** (up to 7 people, one car).
- Runs 5.5 km before meeting the Sana. ~20 m wide, knee-deep, stony bottom.
- **~80% grayling**, many over 50 cm — the highest ratio in the region. Brown
  trout in the same water, to double figures.
- The Pliva permit covers the Ribnik and the Sana too.

## Safety (this wording is deliberate — use it as written)

- The Pliva is a spring creek at **8 °C year-round**. It is too cold for
  swimming. No swimming outside the property.
- **Life vests mandatory for everyone, on the water, at all times.**
- Wetsuit and buoyancy aid essential for wading — we provide both.
- Light rafting is summer only, **age 10+**, with adult supervision and a
  safety briefing.
- The river is cold enough to cause cold-water shock in under a minute.
  These rules belong in every booking confirmation, repeated at check-in, and
  posted in the dining hall.
- Catch and release. Travel insurance recommended.

### The licence — the thing every guest asks about

The fishing licence is **€40 per day on the Pliva and €50 per day on the Ribnik — €35 a day on the Ribnik in November and December**
(Milka, 3 Nov 2026). Pliva sits at the lower end; the Ribnik days cost more.
It is shown separately because there is no hidden fee: the package price
already absorbs it, and room-only guests are told what it costs and that we
will buy it for them.

**Not every guest is an angler.** Many come for the river, the food and the
quiet, and would never buy a licence. So the site offers a toggle on the rates
section — *Room and meals only* (the default) and *Including the fishing
licence* — and the licence price is stated on its own line either way. The
default is off: a licence-inclusive total would mislead a guest who is still
deciding whether to fish at all.

When writing copy that quotes a total, say which version it is. "€162 a fishing
day" means with the Pliva licence; "€122 a day" means room and meals only.

Frame it as a value, not a cost — it funds river conservation, it is the same
rate every operator in the market pays, and catch-and-release is mandatory.

### The tourist tax (confirmed by Milka, 3 Nov 2026)

On room-only stays the tax is still charged on arrival and itemised on the

## The rules, which matter more than the words

1. **Never open with a price.** Open with the property, the river, and why
   you are writing to *them* specifically.
2. **Be a person.** "I run a small riverside guesthouse" — not "we are a
   leading fly-fishing destination".
3. **Ask for a conversation, not a booking.** "Can we talk?" is a much
   easier yes than "Can you send me guests?"
4. **One message, then leave them alone.** A single polite follow-up a week
   later, then nothing.
5. **Never send the same message to everyone.** The first line must be about
   *them* — their week, their river, their post.
6. **Do not ask for commission from a guide you have not worked with.** Offer
   the standard 10% and free accommodation for the host. Ask for a
   partnership, not a percentage, and let them propose the number.
7. **Attach three photos, not a link.** A terrace, the dining hall, the
   water. A link goes in the body, after the first line, and never as the
   only thing in the message.

**Cadence: 2–3 DMs a week.** Not 30 in one afternoon. The fishing community
is small and connected; a burst of messages is how you get ignored by
everyone at once.

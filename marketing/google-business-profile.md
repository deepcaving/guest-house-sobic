## 6. Q&A — pre-seed these (you can ask and answer as the owner)

**Q: Do you speak English?**
> The host speaks Serbian and Bosnian. We handle English, German, Bosnian and
> Italian by WhatsApp before you arrive — the booking process, your
> confirmation and the orientation material are all in your language. The site
> is available in English, Deutsch, Bosanski and Italiano.

**Q: Can you arrange airport transfers?**
> Yes — door to door in a private car or minivan. From Sarajevo (about 4
> hours, our default, included in the fishing packages), Banja Luka (about 2
> hours, the closest airport) or Split (about 3.5 hours including the border
> crossing). Tell us your flight and we will be waiting with a name board.

**Q: Is the water safe to swim in?**
> No. The Pliva is a spring creek at 8 °C year-round — far too cold for
> swimming, and it can cause cold-water shock in under a minute. We do not
> swim here. We arrange light rafting on the calm upper reaches in summer for
> guests aged 10 and over, with life vests, a safety briefing and adult
> supervision.

**Q: Can I bring my dog?**
> Yes, with prior approval. There is a resident dog at the house who is
> friendly with other dogs.

**Q: Do you offer fly-fishing packages?**
> Yes. We work with the country's established English-speaking guides and
> arrange the full package: room, full board, daily guided fishing, the
> fishing licence and the airport transfer. The Pliva Week — 7 nights, 6
> fishing days — is from €1,390 per person for a group of four. Message us
> with your dates.

**Q: How far is the property from the Pliva springs?**
> About 2 km downstream of the Pliva spring. The upper Pliva fly-fishing beat
> is a 5-minute drive, and the Tourist Complex Pliva at the source is 8 km
> away. Jajce is 45 minutes by car.

**Q: What is your cancellation policy?**
> Full refund more than 60 days before arrival, 50% refund 30–60 days before,
> no refund inside 30 days. We strongly recommend travel insurance.

**Q: Is the fishing licence included in the price?**
> Yes, in the fishing packages — the Pliva permit is €50 per day for
> non-resident guests and we buy it for you, so there is nothing extra to pay
> on arrival. If you stay on a room-only basis we arrange the licence for you
> at the same rate. Catch and release is mandatory on the Pliva.

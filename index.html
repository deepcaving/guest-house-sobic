Welcome to the lodge

A house that happens to sit on the water

Most fishing lodges on the Pliva put you in a bungalow with a view of the river. We put you on the river. The dining hall opens onto a lawn that drops straight into the water; the terrace sofas sit above the bank; the kitchen has a wood-fire grill and the river runs past the window.

The fishing

The Pliva is a spring creek, and it stays that way

Three springs emerge at Pljeva and merge into the Pliva. It runs 33 km through the Šipovo valley and joins the Janj, then the Vrbas at Jajce. Because the water comes out of the ground at a constant 8 °C, the river never floods, never goes turbid, and never stops feeding.

  • Wild brown trout and grayling to 60 cm, catch and release only
  • Gin-clear water — you sight fish, then you cast at what you saw
  • Long, fine leaders (often over 5 m) and small flies: size 18–22
  • Upper Pliva open year-round; the Jajce beat opens March to September
  • Seven rivers within reach: Pliva, Janj, Ribnik, Sana, Sanica, Una, Krušnica

On the water

Wade the Pliva, or fish it from the bank

The Pliva is shallow enough to wade most of its length — knee to thigh deep over gravel, with a steady current that holds the line against you. It is the kind of water where a long leader pays: cast upstream, let it drift, and the trout will tell you where it is. For those who prefer to stay dry, the lodge bank and the meadow give you casting room to the same fish.

  • Wading — thigh-deep, gravel bottom, strong current
  • Bank casting — from the lodge terrace and the meadow flat
  • Guided days — a local guide, waders and rods included

The water is 8 °C all year. A wetsuit and a buoyancy aid are essential, and we provide both. Life vests are worn at all times on the guided days.

A second river, an hour away

The Ribnik: bigger grayling, an hour from the door

Fifty kilometres north-west of the lodge the Ribnik runs for five and a half kilometres before it meets the Sana. It is short, shallow and absurdly clear — a true chalk stream, barely 20 metres wide, knee-deep at most, with the stony bottom visible in every cast. Where the Pliva gives you big grayling that scrutinise every fly, the Ribnik gives you more of them: roughly eighty percent of the fish here are grayling, and they run to 50 cm and beyond. Brown trout live in the same water and reach double figures.

  • 50 km, about an hour from the lodge by car
  • 80% grayling, many over 50 cm — the highest ratio anywhere in the region
  • Shallow and easy — 20 m wide, knee-deep, crossable on foot almost anywhere
  • Same licence — your Pliva permit covers the Ribnik and the Sana as well
